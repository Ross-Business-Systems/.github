# Ross Business Systems

**Practical software, built with evidence, automation and maintainability in mind.**

Ross Business Systems is a founder-led software organisation building tools for education, internal operations and software delivery.

Our work is guided by a simple principle:

> **Automate. Modernise. Smarten up.**

## What we build

We focus on software that removes repetitive work, makes operational state easier to understand, and turns fragmented processes into dependable systems.

Current areas of development include:

- **Education technology** — admissions, enrolment, planning and operational workflow software.
- **Developer operations** — project intelligence, engineering evidence, delivery visibility and internal tooling.
- **Founder systems** — lightweight operational software designed to scale from a solo founder into a growing organisation.
- **Reusable engineering foundations** — project templates, conventions and delivery workflows for reliable .NET development.

## Engineering approach

We prefer systems that are:

- **Evidence-backed** — source systems remain authoritative and important claims stay traceable.
- **Automation-first** — repeated manual work is a candidate for removal.
- **Operationally boring** — predictable deployment, recovery and observability beat unnecessary novelty.
- **Secure by default** — least privilege, bounded integrations and explicit trust boundaries.
- **Maintainable** — clear architecture, durable documentation and small, reviewable delivery slices.
- **Honest about uncertainty** — unknown, unavailable and incomplete data remain visible rather than being converted into false precision.

## How we work

Ross Business Systems uses an agent-assisted engineering workflow built around human ownership and repository-verifiable evidence.

Work is delivered in bounded slices with:

1. a canonical backlog item;
2. isolated implementation;
3. automated tests and verification;
4. pull-request review;
5. explicit remediation of findings;
6. current-head CI;
7. squash merge;
8. integrated review for coherent packages;
9. controlled deployment and operational verification.

AI agents accelerate implementation and review, but repository state, tests, source evidence and human decisions remain authoritative.
